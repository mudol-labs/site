“Camera failed” and “Can't connect to camera”: how to fix it

“Warning: Camera failed” on Samsung phones, “Can't connect to the camera”, a black screen in the camera, or a camera app that keeps crashing are common on Android. When the cause is software, you can often fix it yourself.

Work through these steps from easy to hard. Stop as soon as the camera works again.

  1. Restart the phone

    Hold the power button and choose Restart. This clears many temporary camera service glitches. If storage is almost full or the phone is very hot, free up space or let it cool down first.

  2. Clear the Camera app's cache and storage

    Open Settings › Apps, pick the Camera app, open Storage and tap Clear cache. If that doesn't help, tap Clear data (on Samsung phones: Clear storage or Clear data). This resets camera settings but does not delete your photos and videos. Menu names vary a little between brands.

  3. Check permissions and apps that are holding the camera

    In the Camera app's Permissions, make sure Camera is allowed. If a video call app, flashlight or QR scanner is holding the camera, the Camera app may fail to open. Fully close those apps from the recent apps screen, then open the camera again.

  4. Use safe mode to find a conflicting app

    Safe mode temporarily turns off the apps you installed. On most phones you long-press the power-off icon on the power menu to reach it, though the steps vary by model. If the camera works in safe mode, one of your apps is the cause. Remove recently installed apps first, especially camera filter, effects or screen recorder apps, one at a time. If it fails in safe mode too, an app is probably not the cause.

  5. Update the system and apps

    In Settings › Software update (the name varies by brand), check for a new version, and update the Camera app in the Play Store. Camera errors are sometimes fixed by updates.

  6. Test each lens

    In the Camera app, switch between the front and rear cameras, and between ultra-wide, main and telephoto. If the black screen or crash only happens with one lens, that lens or its part may be the problem. Trying a different camera app also tells you whether the default Camera app is at fault. Some phones also have a built-in diagnostics tool from the manufacturer that can test the camera.

  7. If it is hardware, go to a service center

    If nothing above helps and the camera also fails in safe mode, it is likely hardware. This is especially true if only the ultra-wide lens fails, it works on and off, or it started after a drop or water damage. Get it checked at a service center, and back up your photos and videos first.

FAQ

Why does my camera suddenly show a black screen?

Often it is software: a Camera app glitch, another app using the camera, or a temporary system error. Restarting and clearing the Camera app's cache fixes many cases. If it keeps happening and also fails in safe mode, it could be hardware such as the camera module.

Will clearing the Camera app's data delete my photos?

No. Clearing the Camera app's data resets its settings. Photos and videos saved in your gallery stay. It is still wise to back up important files first.

My camera works on and off. Is it broken?

It can be software, but if the steps above don't stop it, hardware is likely. Get it checked at a service center, especially if it began after a drop or water exposure.

Does CamRescue repair a broken camera?

No. CamRescue can't repair hardware. It helps you capture photos from a failing camera in the moments it works. Repair or replacement has to be done by a service center.
